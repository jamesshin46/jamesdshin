# 01 — Product brief

## One-liner
A personal portfolio for **James Shin** that feels like browsing an artist's page in a
music-streaming app: James is the "artist," each part of his life is an **album**, and each
project, role highlight, or performance is a **track**.

## Who it's for
Primary: recruiters and hiring managers for **product manager roles at media, entertainment, and
gaming companies** (music streaming, video platforms, game studios). Secondary: classmates,
collaborators, friends.

What they should feel in 10 seconds:
1. "This person gets consumer media products" — the interface itself proves it.
2. "Real impact, real audience" — numbers like $10M, 100K+ views, 2.5M+ views, live YouTube counts.
3. "I know who he is as a person" — dance, music, cafés, food.

Tone: **polished, confident, warm, a little playful.** It should feel like a well-made product, not
a fan site. K-pop is one part of James's story (dance performance, creator/community growth), not
the theme of the whole site. Keep visuals clean and professional.

## The concept, mapped
| Streaming-app idea | On James's site |
|---|---|
| Artist page | The home screen: headshot, name, tagline, headline stat, action buttons |
| Time-based greeting | "Good evening" etc. above the header, based on the visitor's local time |
| Popular tracks | James's top 5 highlights with a metric as the "play count" |
| Discography (albums) | Each part of his life: Strategy&, Xing Fu Tang, Methix, Paul Weiss (upcoming), KNESIS, F4MX, Asian Cultural Union, Beta Alpha Psi, NYU |
| Tracks | Individual accomplishments / projects / dance covers inside each album |
| Play button on a cover | Opens the YouTube video in a modal; the now-playing bar updates |
| Play button on a work track | Opens a short detail card; the now-playing bar updates |
| Live play counts | Real YouTube view counts, refreshed hourly by a GitHub Action |
| Now-playing bar | Persistent bottom bar: what James is doing right now, or what the visitor just played |
| Year-end recap | A **"Recap"** button on the artist page opens a full-screen, tap-through story of James's highlights |
| Search | Real, instant search across albums, tracks, interests, and places |
| "Fans also like" | James's interests as circular artist-style chips; **Cafés** and **Asian Cuisine** open an interactive map of his recommendations with personal notes |
| Artist bio | About section |
| Follow / message | Contact links + résumé download |

## Signature moments (get these right)
1. **The artist page header** — the first impression. Big, confident, with the live total-views counter.
2. **Recap** — the shareable, delightful moment.
3. **Playing a dance cover** — the video opens, the now-playing bar animates to that track, the
   live view count sits next to it.
4. **The café / food map** — a personal, human touch that makes people remember him.

## Out of scope (do not build)
- Case studies, product teardowns, or pitches.
- Blog, CMS, login, comments, analytics dashboards.
- Background music or autoplaying audio.
